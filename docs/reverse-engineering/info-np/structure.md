## 2. Cover design pattern

Stable formula 2023–early 2025 (NP1-23 p.1 … NP3-25 p.1):
- Top thin band: "Ausgabe X · Monat 20YY · www.medizinonline.ch/com"
- Single full-bleed photo (mostly iStock; credit on TOC page: "Titelbild: …, iStock")
- 3-column teaser block titled by section: **CME-FORTBILDUNG**, **Medizin**, **Praxismanagement**. Each teaser = topic kicker (bold) + 1–2-line headline.
- Logo "InFo Neurologie & Psychiatrie" wordmark.
- 6–8 teasers per cover.

URL evolution: medizinonline.ch on 2023 issues; switched to medizinonline.com from NP3-23 onward (NP3-23 p.1).

Disruption from NP1-26: page 1 is a full-page Kesimpta ad, page 3 carries only the Ausgabe-band (a "false cover"), editorial moves to printed p.3 (=PDF p.5), TOC to printed p.4 (=PDF p.6). NP4-25 also lacks the standard TOC frame (ed. on p.3, but the teaser cover persists on p.1). So the strict cover pattern survives NP4-25 but breaks in NP1-26.

## 3. TOC pattern

Title "INHALTSVERZEICHNIS"; sections in CAPS section heads in fixed order: EDITORIAL → CME-FORTBILDUNG → (SONDERREPORT/PUBLIREPORTAGE) → MEDIZIN → PRAXISMANAGEMENT → WEITERE RUBRIKEN.
Each entry: page number (left), kicker line + headline (1–2 lines), author/affiliation in italics (where applicable).
Bottom block "WEITERE RUBRIKEN" lists scattered short items (News, Board, Auf einen Blick, Publireportage, Impressum, sometimes "Das Letzte") with comma-separated page lists like "4, 5, 30, 33, 38" (NP1-25 p.4).
Footer: "Titelbild: <credit>, iStock" (always).

## 4. Editorial pattern

- Always 1 full page (printed p.1).
- Header bar: "Die Fortbildungsthemen in dieser Ausgabe:" + 2–3 dotted page-references to the CME articles + the CME-Fragen page. Plus mini-banner "Credits auf medizinonline.com — Einloggen, Fragen beantworten und direkt CME-Zertifkat downloaden." Identical wording in every issue 2023→2026.
- Kicker (small, e.g. "Subdurales Hämatom") + bold 1-line headline (e.g. "Tod eines Heiligen", NP3-23 p.3).
- ~350–500 words of essayistic commentary; opens with a black square ▪ glyph; usually a vignette (cultural/historical/news anecdote) leading to a thematic bridge into the issue.
- Signature line, no portrait photo, no contact email (until 2025), no signature graphic.
- 2023–2024: signed by **Leoni Burggraf, Redaktion** in every issue (NP1-23 to NP6-24).
- 2025: handover to **Tanja Schliebe, Chefredaktion** from NP2-25 onward (NP1-25 still Burggraf; NP2-25 p.3, NP3-25 p.3, NP4-25 p.3 = Schliebe).
- Late 2025–2026: a third voice **Dr. Ulf Blanke, Redaktion** signs NP5-25, NP6-25, NP1-26. Tone shifts from earnest essay (Burggraf) to academic (Schliebe) to wry/colloquial (Blanke, e.g. "biokular schauen"; NP6-25 p.3).
- From NP1-26 the editorial gains an author email block on the first CME piece (e.g. ulf.blanke@primemedic.ch on NP1-26 p.8).

## 7. Page-budget arithmetic

- All 19 issues observed are divisible by 4 (40, 44, 48, 52, 56). Confirmed via mdls page counts.
- Modal length 44 pp (12/19 issues). Median 44.
- Estimated mix per 44-page issue (from NP3-25 sample): editorial 1 + TOC 1 + CME 12 + Medizin ~18 + Sonderreport/Publireportage ~2 + Praxismanagement ~3 + News/Board/Auf einen Blick ~2 + Impressum ½ + Das Letzte ½ + ads (covers + interior) **~6 pages = ~14% advertising**. Cross-checked NP1-23: ads on p.2, p.43, p.44 + interior pharma sponsorship ≈ 4–5 pp = ~10–12 %. Counting Sonderreport / Publireportage / Branchen News (sponsored editorial) plus pure ads, advertising-influenced share is **~25–30 %** of total pages; pure unpaid editorial **~70–75 %**.

## 8. Open questions for the human editor

1. **Author photos vs. no photos**: external CME authors sometimes get photos, sometimes not — is this a rights/availability issue or an editorial rule?
2. **Volume numbering**: Vol. 21 = 2023 implies the magazine started in 2003. Confirm founding date and whether the brand was always called "InFo Neurologie & Psychiatrie".
3. **Sonderreport vs. Publireportage vs. Branchen News**: the rate-card distinction (price, length, disclosure language) is not visible from the PDFs. The Mediadaten document would clarify.
4. **CME accreditation body**: which Schweizer Fachgesellschaft awards the credits referenced ("Credits auf medizinonline.com")? Not stated in any sampled imprint.
5. **Bilingual policy**: NP6-24 p.14 has a French CME contribution. Is French now permanent or one-off? No French observed in 2025/2026 sample.
6. **NP1-26 cover redesign**: the ad-as-page-1 is a major brand decision (Kesimpta full-page replaces the teaser cover; the actual cover frame appears truncated on p.3). Is this a rate-card change selling U1 to advertisers, or a one-off?
7. **CME 2 → 1 reduction**: NP5-25 and NP1-26 only carry one CME article ("Das Fortbildungsthema in dieser Ausgabe"). Is this the new normal or a content-shortfall workaround?
8. **Chefredaktion succession**: 3 different signing authors within 12 months (Burggraf → Schliebe → Blanke). Internal handover order and current responsibilities not deducible from PDFs.
9. **"Auf einen Blick"**: graphic/infographic format dropped from NP3-25 onwards (last appearance NP1-25 p.33 Praxismanagement-adjacent). Confirm whether retired.
10. **Themenplan NP3-26**: the .docx was not opened. It almost certainly resolves several of the above (planned page count, planned section list, advertising slots).
