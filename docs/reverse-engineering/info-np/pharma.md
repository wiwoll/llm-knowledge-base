- **Biogen** — 14/19 issues. Products: **Tysabri/Natalizumab (s.c.)** (NP3-24 back cover; NP6-23 Biogen-funded reprint), **Vumerity/Diroximelfumarat**, **Skyclarys/Omaveloxolon** (Friedreich-Ataxia; appears as Publireportage NP3-25 p.28-29 and as Sonderreport NP1-26 p.28-29 + NP5-25 p.41+).

**Tier-2 (regular but not every issue):** Novartis (Kesimpta/Ofatumumab), Janssen-Cilag (Spravato/Esketamin; Trevicta/Xeplion - paliperidonpalmitat; latter distributed in CH by **OM Pharma Suisse**), Lundbeck (Vyepti/Eptinezumab), Eli Lilly (Emgality + CME sponsoring), Bial (Ongentys/Opicapon + CME sponsoring), BMS (Zeposia/Ozanimod), Sanofi (Aubagio/Teriflunomid; MS Publireportage NP6-25 p.39), Jazz Pharmaceuticals (sleep/narcolepsy: Wakix, Sunosi, Xywav), Merck KGaA (Mavenclad/Cladribin), Teva (Ajovy), UCB (Vimpat/Briviact).

**Tier-3 occasional:** Pfizer, AbbVie, Lundbeck, Novo Nordisk (Wegovy/Semaglutide — yes, an obesity drug appears in this neuro/psych journal as Publireportage in NP6-24 p.33), Idorsia (Quviviq/Daridorexant), Almirall, Eisai (LEQEMBI), Stoke Therapeutics, Recordati, Neuraxpharm.

Pattern: Swiss-market local affiliates dominate the display-ad slots (Roche Pharma (Schweiz) AG, Biogen Switzerland AG / Baar, Janssen-Cilag AG / Zug, Novartis Pharma Schweiz, OM Pharma Suisse / Villars-sur-Glâne); German parent entities (Merck KGaA, BMS GmbH, Neuraxpharm Arzneimittel GmbH, Roche Pharma AG) appear mainly as sources for **Markt & Medizin** items, often re-sourced from German society congresses (DGN, DGPPN). **Estimated 4–8 distinct pharma sponsors per issue** (display ads + Sonderreport/Publireportage + Markt & Medizin items).

**Visual differentiation:** PUBLIREPORTAGE / SONDERREPORT label is set in capitals, top of page, in a coloured strip distinct from grey "MEDIZIN" / "NEWS" header. The footer always carries a Kurzfachinformation block in tiny type with the company address + clearance / approval code (e.g. `Biogen-274461_10.2025`, `MAT-CH-2501490_10/2025`, `CH_CP-392592_v1.0`, `CH-TRE-25004.07/2025`).

**Tone:** Sponsored items are clearly promotional. They name the brand product (always with ®/™), foreground efficacy data, and use "Take-Home" or "Wussten Sie schon …?" boxes ending in product call-out (NP6-25 p.34 OM Pharma: "Paliperidonpalmitat (Xeplion®) reduziert das Mortalitätsrisiko um 89 %"). Author affiliations are stated; **explicit COI / disclosure of speaker fees is NOT printed** — only the sponsor relationship to the article.

Off-label is occasionally hinted (Ginkgo biloba "off-label bei beginnender Demenz" in NP6-24 editorial; sponsored items stay on-label). Reimbursement context is a recurring anchor (Spravato Spezialitätenliste limitatio; Skyclarys "kassenzulässig seit 1. Mai 2025"; Trevicta Spezialitätenliste).

## 4. News / Markt & Medizin

The **MARKT & MEDIZIN** band sits in late-issue NEWS pages (p.33-37 typically). It is **not editorial** — every item carries a tag line `MARKT & MEDIZIN <CompanyName>`. Examples: Merck KGaA (Mavenclad), Jazz Pharmaceuticals, Eli Lilly (Suisse) SA, Biogen, Roche, Bial, Sanofi, Teva, UCB, Bristol Myers Squibb, Neuraxpharm, Zambon.

- **Source pattern:** items are paraphrases or near-translations of pharma press releases. Sources cited: "Quelle: Roche's fenebrutinib shows unprecedented positive Phase III results …, 10.11.2025" (NP6-25); "Quelle: Biogen and Stoke Therapeutics Present New Data … 9.10.2025"; "Quelle: Fortschritte in der Therapie der MS: Neue Daten vom DGN-Kongress, 11.11.2024".
- **Tone:** broadly neutral but product-positive. No competitor balancing. Brand names are written prominently (LEQEMBI IQLIK™, Zorevunersen, Fenebrutinib).
- **Length:** ~250-450 words per item, 2-3 items per page, often with a small "summary box" sidebar.
- **Company always named** in the tag; product almost always named, even when a development stage (e.g. Zorevunersen ASO, in-development).
- **Pricing/reimbursement** mentioned when relevant ("kassenzulässig", Spezialitätenliste). **Regulatory** mentions are routine: Swissmedic approvals, EMA/FDA approvals, label expansions.

## 5. "Doctor's choice" / reader-favoured content signals

The publication exposes **no explicit reader-engagement instrumentation**: no "Most read", no "Top 10", no Letters to the editor / Leserbriefe section, no reader-poll write-ups, no "Editor's pick" badges, no online-traffic ranking reprinted in the PDF.

Indirect signals:
- **CME-Fortbildung on p.6-18 of every issue** is the prestige slot — these articles attract authors with "Prof. Dr. med." titles, are tied to credit-bearing tests on medizinonline.com, and topic recurrence proxies sustained reader interest. Recurring CME themes: **MS therapy management** (every year), **Alzheimer biomarkers / Lecanemab** (every 2024-2025 issue), **Parkinson L-Dopa / device-aided therapy**, **migraine prophylaxis (CGRP class)**, **Epilepsie post-Schlaganfall** (NP6-24), **Schizophrenie / LAI** (NP6-25), **gerontopsychiatrie / Polypharmazie** (NP6-23).
- **Front-of-book editorials** by Leoni Burggraf (Redaktion) often address gender medicine, crisis/resilience, touch medicine — softer themes that contrast with hard-pharma editorial.
- **Sticky multi-year topics** (high recurrence across 2023-2025): MS DMTs (every issue), CGRP/migraine prophylaxis, ALS gene therapy/Tofersen, Alzheimer anti-amyloid (Lecanemab), insomnia/orexin antagonists (Daridorexant/Quviviq), narcolepsy (Pitolisant/Solriamfetol), sleep medicine generally.
- **Article placement as proxy:** the front-of-book CME slot is the perceived high-interest zone; a topic recurring in CME slot 2-3 years in a row indicates demand. MS, Alzheimer, Parkinson dominate.
- **Surveys cited but not reader surveys** — clinical surveys quoted in editorial (Online-Umfragen unter MS-Patienten/Ärzten in NP1-24, NP1-25, NP2-24, NP3-25). No reader-feedback infrastructure visible.

## 6. Compliance & disclosure norms

- **No individual-author conflict-of-interest declarations** on editorial articles (zero hits on "Interessenkonflikt", "disclosure" beyond reference titles). The journal does not print COI footers.
- **Brand-neutral guideline coverage in editorial sections** is mostly maintained: editorial uses generic + brand format (e.g. "Ocrelizumab" with occasional "(Ocrevus®)"), or generic only. CME articles cite EAN/AAN guidelines without brand emphasis.
- **Boundary between Markt & Medizin and editorial coverage**: the same drug (e.g. Lecanemab, Ocrelizumab) is covered in both — editorial places it in clinical context with comparator data; Markt & Medizin lifts pharma press releases. The label distinction is the only differentiator. There is no rule against editorial covering a drug currently advertised in the same issue.
- **CME sponsorship is allowed but mandates an editorial-freedom statement.** The standard wording (NP1-23 Bial CME, NP6-24 Lilly CME, NP5-24 Lilly):
  > "Dank des Sponsorings der Firma [Bial / Lilly] ist die Teilnahme an dieser Fortbildung für Sie kostenlos. Die Fortbildung ist von Fachleuten erstellt und unter unbedingter Wahrung unserer redaktionellen Freiheit erstellt. Zu keinem Zeitpunkt hatte und hat die Firma Einfluss auf die Inhalte der Fortbildung. Alle Texte unterliegen lediglich der wissenschaftlichen Hoheit der Autoren und der Redaktion von PPM MEDIC."

  French parallel: "Grâce au sponsoring de la société Lilly … dans le respect absolu de notre liberté éditoriale …" (NP6-24 p.21).

## 7. Editorial-commercial firewall — strength

**Strong markers:** capitalised PUBLIREPORTAGE / SONDERREPORT banner, sponsor name in footer + clearance code, embedded Kurzfachinformation, cross-reference to ad page elsewhere in issue ("Kurzfachinformation auf Seite 27"). Medical-writing agency named when used (Cactus Life Sciences). Publishing house named on Sonderreport copyright (© Prime Public Media AG) — i.e. PPM owns the sponsored output, not the sponsor.

**Weaker markers:** MARKT & MEDIZIN items are essentially press-release rewrites but receive only the inline `MARKT & MEDIZIN <Sponsor>` tag, no "Anzeige" word, no Kurzfachinformation. A casual reader could mistake them for editorial NEWS items. Editorial NEWS items in the same NEWS section are signed `(ub)`, `(lb)`, `(mp)` (staff initials) which is the only differentiator.

**Blurred lines:** Sanofi NP6-25 p.39 Publireportage is a KOL Q&A interview that visually resembles an editorial expert interview; only the PUBLIREPORTAGE label and tiny `MAT-CH-2501490` code identify it. NP3-23 Janssen Spravato Publireportage is a patient case-report, format identical to a clinical case article.

## 8. Take-aways for production (rules Claude must follow)

1. **Mandatory labels** (verbatim): use "PUBLIREPORTAGE" or "SONDERREPORT" in capitals at the top of any sponsored page; reserve "MARKT & MEDIZIN [Company Name]" as a tag line for press-release derived NEWS items.
2. **Sponsor footer** must always include: company legal name, Swiss address (Neuhofstrasse 30, CH-6340 Baar etc.), and a clearance/approval code in the form `<Company>-<Number>_<MM.YYYY>` or `MAT-CH-<digits>_<MM/YYYY>` or `CH_CP-<digits>_v<x>.<y>`.
3. **Funding-disclosure formulas** (use one of):
   - "Mit freundlicher Unterstützung der Firma X" (lightweight, for reprints)
   - "Dieser Beitrag wurde finanziert von X" (Sonderreport)
   - "Dieser Artikel entstand mit finanzieller Unterstützung von X" (Publireportage)
   - For CME: full editorial-freedom paragraph (see §6) is mandatory, in same language as article.
4. **Medical-writing CRO disclosure** when used: "Die Medical-Writing-Unterstützung wurde durch [Author], [Author] ([CRO Name]) im Auftrag von [Sponsor] erbracht."
5. **Pharma-name handling**:
   - Editorial: prefer INN (generic), brand parenthetical only at first mention. Multi-brand neutrality where possible.
   - Sponsored: brand name with ® / ™ throughout, INN in parenthesis.
   - Markt & Medizin: brand name allowed prominently because press-release derivative.
6. **Kurzfachinformation embedding**: every product mentioned in a sponsored block needs a Kurzfachinformation either same page or referenced ("Kurzfachinformation auf Seite X"); fields Z (Zusammensetzung) / I (Indikation) / D (Dosierung) / KI / VM / IA / SS/St / UAW / P / Abgabekat. / Stand der Information.
7. **Markt & Medizin neutrality**: 250-450 words per item, neutral-positive tone, source line "Quelle: <press-release title>, <date>"; brand allowed; no comparative slamming; cite primary references.
8. **Off-label**: never advanced in sponsored content. Editorial may discuss off-label only in clinical-context articles, not in product-tied pieces.
9. **Data claims**: every efficacy/safety claim in sponsored content must carry a numbered citation [n] resolved in the article's reference list, with primary trial naming (MOXIe, STEP, ULTIMATE, etc.). Studie-Endpunkte and p-values to be quoted exactly as in source publication.
10. **KOL attribution**: name + affiliation + "Die hier getätigten Aussagen spiegeln die professionelle Meinung von [Name] wider" disclaimer when using interview format. (No personal COI of KOL printed — current journal practice — but Claude should consider this a known gap.)
11. **Reprint / co-publication chain**: Sonderreport reprints from BrainMag / medEdition Verlag / ECTRIMS Special carry both `© medEdition Verlag GmbH, Hirzel <year>. Abdruck mit freundlicher Genehmigung des Verlags.` and `Erstpublikation erschienen in BrainMag <year>; <issue>: <pages>` lines.
12. **Charity/society Anzeigen** (Multiple Sklerose Gesellschaft, Sucht Schweiz, Epi-Suisse, Demenzliga) are tagged with the explicit word "Anzeige" in fine print at the corner — standard quarter-page format with PC-Konto donation line.
13. **Inside-front and back covers** are premium pharma slots; **inside-back is congress / society** placement (likely patronage/free-rate); editorial-board page (with Editorial-Board listing + Patronage line) sits late in book just before back-cover ad.
